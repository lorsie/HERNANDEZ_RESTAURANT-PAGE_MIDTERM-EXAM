About

Croissant Noir Cafe

Nestled in the heart of the city, Croissant Noir Café is your cozy escape for handcrafted pastries, rich coffee, and comforting bites. Inspired by French elegance and modern charm, we serve flaky croissants, indulgent treats, and savory favorites—all made fresh daily. Whether you're stopping by for a quick espresso or a lazy brunch, you'll always find warmth, flavor, and friendly faces.
